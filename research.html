Research Portfolios

Five research portfolios, each built around experimental platforms.

My work focuses on experimental thermal-fluid systems, with research programs spanning additive manufacturing, electronics cooling, heat pipes, atmospheric water harvesting, and porous media transport.

Core themeExperimental thermal-fluid systems

Research portfolios

Each portfolio below groups related projects, platforms, and outputs. The goal is to show how the work evolved over time without forcing unrelated items into the same bucket.

01. Experimental Thermal Engineering

2016–2018 • Arizona State University • M.S. research under Prof. Patrick Phelan

Early experimental work on 3D printed heat exchangers, additive manufacturing, and thermal characterization.

Experimental platform: Heat exchanger test setup

02. Advanced Thermal Materials

2018–2019 • Arizona State University • Early Ph.D. research under Prof. Beomjin Kwon

Research on thermal conductivity of coated polymer foams, transient plane source measurements, and thermal transport modeling.

Experimental platform: TPS thermal conductivity setup

03. Electronics Cooling (GCTG)

2022–Present • Global Cooling Technology Group • Lead, Thermal Engineering

Industrial research and development on closed-loop pulsating heat pipes, thermal testing automation, fixture design, and electronics cooling validation.

Experimental platform: Pulsating heat pipe test systems

04. NASA Heat Pipe Technologies

2022–2025 • Arizona State University • Ph.D. research under Prof. Patrick Phelan

Permeability characterization of additively manufactured wick structures for heat pipe radiators, including graded wicks and TPMS-based porous media.

Experimental platform: Permeability characterization platform

05. Atmospheric Water Harvesting

2023–Present • GCWT / AWII • Ph.D., postdoctoral, and commercialization work

Desiccant assessment, ultrasound-enhanced desorption, thermoelectric extensions, coated desiccants, and prototype development for low-humidity water harvesting.

Experimental platforms: Desiccant assessment, ultrasound regeneration, thermoelectric AWH

Research identity

The common thread across these portfolios is experimental characterization of transport phenomena in engineered porous structures and thermal management systems.

Method strengths

  • Experimental design and instrumentation
  • Thermal-fluid testing and validation
  • Uncertainty analysis
  • Automation and data processing
  • Model-to-experiment comparison

Experimental platforms

These are the reusable systems that support multiple projects and publications.

Confined jet flow visualization platform

Built for undergraduate experimental fluid mechanics and flow asymmetry studies.

Heat exchanger test platform

Developed for master’s research on 3D printed heat exchangers.

TPS thermal conductivity platform

Used for coated polymer foam thermal conductivity measurements and methodology development.

Permeability characterization platform

Used across dissertation chapters on LPBF wicks, graded structures, and TPMS cells.

Desiccant assessment platform

Used to compare beaded, powdered, and coated desiccants for atmospheric water harvesting.

Ultrasound regeneration platform

Used to test ultrasound-assisted desorption and energy reduction for AWH.

Thermoelectric AWH platform

Used in the thermoelectric extension of the AWH research program.

Pulsating heat pipe test platforms

Used for electronics cooling validation, automation, and customer-facing testing at GCTG.
