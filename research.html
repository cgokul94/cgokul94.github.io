Research Portfolios

Experimental thermal-fluid systems organized into five research portfolios.

My work focuses on experimental characterization, validation, and translation of thermal-fluid systems for electronics cooling, porous media, heat pipes, and atmospheric water harvesting.

Research overview

The same experimental mindset runs through every portfolio: define the transport problem, build the platform, measure the system, validate the results, and translate the work into publications, reports, patents, or industry development.

A common research thread

The portfolios below are grouped by engineering challenge rather than by advisor or publication type. That makes it easier to see how each body of work evolved and which experimental platform supported it.

Experimental design Instrumentation Uncertainty analysis Thermal-fluid testing Model validation

Current activity

Electronics cooling at GCTG, NASA heat pipe permeability studies, and atmospheric water harvesting remain the primary active portfolios, with research continuing to move toward automated testing and technology translation.
