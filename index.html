Research Areas

Two-Phase Cooling

Research on closed-loop pulsating heat pipe systems for high-power electronics cooling, including experimental thermal testing, transport characterization, and development activities through NSF STTR, NSF SBIR, DARPA SBIR, and industry-supported programs.

Atmospheric Water Harvesting

Research on desiccant-based atmospheric water harvesting systems, including coated desiccants, ultrasound-assisted desorption, thermal regeneration methods, and prototype development through GCWT and AWII-supported activities.

Space Thermal Systems

Research activities involving permeability characterization, wick structures, and additively manufactured heat pipe radiators for space thermal management applications under NASA ESI-supported programs.

Thermal Modeling

COMSOL, ANSYS Fluent, MATLAB workflows, and experimental validation for thermal-fluid systems and product optimization.

Biomedical Diagnostics

Thermal modeling and experimental validation activities for point-of-care molecular diagnostic systems.

Additive Manufacturing

Heat exchangers, wick structures, branching thermal hardware, and fabrication-aware design for manufacturable thermal systems.
