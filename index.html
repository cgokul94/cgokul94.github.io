Summary

Mechanical engineer with experience in experimental research, thermal modeling, and industry R&D across thermal-fluid systems, atmospheric water harvesting, and electronics cooling. Work includes federally funded research programs, conference presentations, peer-reviewed publications, and a U.S. provisional patent.

Research Areas

Two-Phase Cooling

Closed-loop pulsating heat pipe systems for high-power electronics cooling, including experimental testing, thermal transport characterization, and devlopment activities through NSF STTR, NSF SBIR, DARPA SBIR, and industry-supported programs.

Atmospheric Water Harvesting

Desiccant-based atmospheric water harvesting systems, including coated desiccants, ultrasound-assisted desorption, thermal regeneration methods, and prototype development through GCWT and AWII-supported activities.

Space Thermal Systems

NASA ESI-supported activities involving permeability characterization, functionally graded wick structures, and additively manufactured heat pipe radiators for space thermal management applications.

Thermal Modeling

COMSOL, ANSYS Fluent, MATLAB workflows, and experimental validation for thermal-fluid systems.

Biomedical Diagnostics

Thermal modeling and experimental validation for point-of-care molecular diagnostic systems for GH Labs NAAToS Platform.
