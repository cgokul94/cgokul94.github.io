My work focuses on translating thermal-fluid science into deployable engineering systems through integrated computational, experimental, and systems-level approaches. I am particularly interested in technologies that bridge fundamental transport phenomena with real-world applications in energy, cooling, diagnostics, aerospace, and environmental systems.
