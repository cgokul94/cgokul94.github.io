Mechanical engineer specializing in experimental thermal-fluid systems, with federally funded research spanning DARPA, NSF, and NASA programs in two-phase cooling and atmospheric water harvesting. Currently Lead, Thermal Engineering at Global Cooling Technology Group, leading experimental evaluation of pulsating heat pipe cooling for high-power electronics. Co-founded and led technical development for an atmospheric water harvesting venture through university and NSF commercialization programs. Every research phase across nine years — from an undergraduate jet-flow rig to today's pulsating heat pipe test systems — has centered on designing the physical apparatus that makes the measurement possible.
