My work focuses on translating thermal-fluid science into deployable engineering systems through integrated computational, experimental, and systems-level approaches. I am particularly interested in technologies that bridge fundamental transport phenomena with real-world applications in energy, cooling, diagnostics, and environmental systems.

Contributed thermal modeling, validation, and optimization workflows for the NAATOS TB molecular diagnostic platform targeting low-cost, point-of-care tuberculosis diagnostics.

Point-of-care molecular diagnostic platform targeting low-cost tuberculosis testing with integrated thermal control and amplification workflows.

  • Developed COMSOL-based thermal models for thermal subsystem optimization
  • Built parametric thermal workflows to optimize heater sizing, thermal coverage, and power requirements
  • Validated simulation models experimentally with results within 6% of system predictions
  • Supported development workflows for next-generation thermal architectures and transient thermal analysis
  • Performed thermal studies under varying environmental boundary conditions and operating scenarios
  • Contributed to optimization of heater design and energy/battery performance

Tools & Methods: COMSOL, MATLAB, experimental validation, transient thermal analysis, thermal optimization
